Timing calibration

Your device takes a moment to hear and process sound. To measure that delay, you'll hear 4 count-in clicks and then 8 more. Pluck your open A string (pizzicato) exactly on each of the last 8 clicks. Hold the violin about an arm's length from the device and keep the room quiet.
